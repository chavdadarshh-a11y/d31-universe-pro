Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Applies to the D31.UNIVERSE web app and its Android app.

What we collect

Account data (email, name), your fitness profile (goal, level, equipment, age, height, weight, sex, activity), workouts, body measurements, nutrition and recovery logs, progress photos you choose to upload, and photos or videos you submit for form analysis. Payment details are handled by Razorpay; we only receive an order ID, payment ID and status.

How we use it

To run the features you use: plans, tracking, calorie targets, AI form analysis and membership. We do not sell your data and we do not use your photos or videos for advertising.

AI form analysis

When you run a form check, the media you select is sent securely to our server and processed by Google Gemini to generate feedback. We store the text result in your account. The live rep counter runs entirely on your device; camera video is never uploaded.

Guest mode & on-device accounts

If you use the app as a guest, or with an account created on this phone (demo mode), your data stays in your browser storage on this device only. Passwords of on-device accounts are salted and hashed and never leave the phone. Clearing browser data deletes it.

D31 Knowledge

Knowledge posts, photos, PDFs and videos are loaded from D31’s public Google Drive folder. Opening them contacts Google’s servers, which may log your IP address like any website.

Storage & security

Signed-in data is stored with Supabase (row-level security: only you can read your rows). Progress photos are kept in a private storage bucket.

Your rights

You can export a JSON backup from Settings → Privacy & data, and delete your account and all its data yourself from Profile → Delete account (cloud accounts are deleted on our server too). To exercise any right, email dpchavda22@gmail.com or use Settings → Contact & support in the app. We respond within 30 days.

Children

D31 is not intended for children under 13. Users aged 13–17 should use the app with a parent or guardian’s consent.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and notify you in the app.

Deleting your account

Open the app → Profile → Delete account and type DELETE to confirm. Accounts created on the phone are removed from the phone immediately; cloud accounts and their stored data (including progress photos) are deleted from our servers. You can also request deletion of your account and data by emailing dpchavda22@gmail.com from your account email; we delete it within 30 days.
